I've been spending a lot of time thinking about what it means to take the combination of RSI + AGI *seriously*. What decisions would one have to make to actually prepare, and what are my actual beliefs on these things that I'd actually be willing to step behind. For example, if I write through this and decide "RSI is inevitable, and AGI is a natural conclusion to that, therefore, to be best prepared i should do X" and am still unwilling to do X, what does that say about my beliefs? 

Anywho, it's important to start with some definitions. 

Some people define AGI to be "machine intelligence can do everything humans can do, but better and more efficiently". I'm not sure that's the best place to start. It brings robotics into the conversation, and I'm not necessarily sure that it's the right place to exist for now. I'm more interested in when the things are strong enough to basically make all "knowledge work" obsolete for humans. This is the case where basically anything that can be done on a computer is done by AI agents. 

So what is really done on a computer? For one, a lot of code gets written. What is the purpose of code? It varies obviously. Some code is written for entertainment (games). Some is written for consumers (balance your bank accounts, social media, etc...)

Hmmm.... That's not really helpful. Going down a rabbit hole here.
